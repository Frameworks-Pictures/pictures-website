About Us

What is Frameworks?


Frameworks Pictures is a production company founded by originally by three high school students. Alex, Luke, and Max have been friends since middle school and have been creating together since 2021.

Frameworks Pictures originally started as a videography business (Frameworks Videography, founded in 2022), providing for hire video coverage of events. As we left high school and got into our fields in college, we realized we'd rather make short films with our friends than film events. In 2025, we officially re-branded as Frameworks Pictures. If you pay attention, you'll still see the old "Frameworks Videography" logo in the credits of some of our earlier films!

One of our core goals with Frameworks has always been providing experience to young filmmakers. We enjoy working with and educating student filmmakers, and we're always proud of the product that we've all worked together to create.



Meet Our Founders




Alexander Gutowski

agutowski@frameworkspictures.org

Alexander Gutowski is a student at Michigan State University (East Lansing, MI) pursuing a Bachelor of Arts degree in Digital Storytelling with a concentration in Multi-Cam Production and a minor in Information Technology. He has been creating videos since 2017. He is an Adobe Certified Professional in Video Design, and an All-MIPA scholarship recipient for the Broadcast catagory. He is currently the Executive Producer and Tech Director for MSU Telecasters, an extracurricular club at Michigan State for students who want to create videos together. He also serves as a Camera Operator for the B1G Network Student U and Co-Director for the Saline Live Video Crew.

Luke Johnson

ljohnson@frameworkspictures.org

Luke Johnson has been creating media since 2021. He's pursuing an education in Television and Digital Media Production at Ithaca College (Ithaca, NY) and is set to graduate in 2027. At Saline High School, he earned multiple honorable mentions and a second-place prize at the Michigan Interscholastic Press Association. He also worked as a tech director and camera operator at Saline Video. He's also directed at the college level, taking classes in Video Production, Television Studio Production, Editing, and Screenwriting, and participating as a writer, camera operator, and Assistant Producer for ICTV shows.
